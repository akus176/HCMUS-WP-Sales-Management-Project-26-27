Midterm submission video checklist

Record one continuous demo against a fresh migrated and seeded database.

1. Show the repository and the six workspaces: Counter, Service board, Menu, Stock room, Customers, and Reports.
2. Show a dine-in order with a customer, table, approved discount, payment, and completion.
3. Show a takeaway order without a customer.
4. Show a delivery order with a saved address, eligible courier, payment, and delivery completion.
5. Show Menu data, a recipe, an add-on, and a deactivated item excluded from new orders.
6. Show supplier receipt posting, stock adjustment with a reason, movement history, and low stock.
7. Show customer loyalty earning, redemption, and cancellation restoration.
8. Show revenue, best-seller, order-channel, and low-stock reports.
9. Show one invalid action and the visible validation or workflow error.
10. Show the final combined test summary: 1,202 passed, zero failed, zero skipped. Explain that the dedicated PostgreSQL CI job runs the Infrastructure tests; the new Windows job selects only the non-PostgreSQL projects.

Before recording:

- Use the current seeded evidence in docs/ui-evidences as the visual reference. If the database is rebuilt before recording, repeat the same migration and seed procedure.
- Confirm no administrator connection string is visible in the recording.
- Use only a demo account and demo data.
- Keep the final video link unlisted and place it in youtube.txt.
