Coffee Shop Management System — midterm feature summary

Completed for the midterm:
- WinUI desktop client with a direct PostgreSQL connection and six workspaces: Counter, Service board, Menu, Stock room, Customers, and Reports.
- Catalog and category management; drink sizes, add-ons, and recipes.
- Customer profiles, delivery addresses, and loyalty history.
- Dine-in, takeaway, and delivery orders; payment, discount approval, cancellation, and fulfillment workflows.
- Inventory, suppliers, purchase receipts, stock adjustments, low-stock views, and movement history.
- Revenue, best-seller, order-channel, and low-stock reports.
- Repeatable demo data with 20 products, 5 categories, 12 customers, and 24 baseline orders.

High-effort work:
- Transactional order confirmation and cancellation with stock and loyalty compensation.
- PostgreSQL migrations, restricted midterm runtime role, and repeatable seed data.
- Automated domain, application, database integration, and WinUI presentation tests.
- Responsive six-workspace desktop UI and native verification captures.

Planned for the final version:
- Client-to-API migration, staff authentication, server-side role authorization, and API tests.
- Optional business-data AI integration.

Evidence and known limits: see docs/midterm-demonstration.md, docs/midterm-test-cases.md, docs/midterm-submission/video-checklist.txt, and README.md. Card and e-wallet tenders are simulated. Midterm staff selection is not final authentication. The final all-workspace visual audit is tracked in GitHub issue #119.
